## 新增与改进

- 设置里的圆环预览现在就是槽位编辑器：点击槽位选中，用 Tab 或方向键移动焦点，回车更换或添加应用，Delete 移除，Esc 取消。
- 把应用拖到空槽会移动过去，拖到已有应用的槽位则两个应用交换位置，槽位编号和预览始终一致。
- 可见位置数和圆环展开时长移到圆环上方，调整位置数只隐藏后面的槽位，调大即可恢复。
- 切换槽位时高亮沿最短方向绕环旋转，快速连续切换会从屏幕上当前的动画位置继续转，不再跳回起点。

## What's New & Improved

- The ring preview in Settings is now the slot editor: click a slot to select it, move focus with Tab or the arrow keys, press Return to replace or add an app, Delete to remove it, and Esc to cancel.
- Drag an app onto an empty slot to move it there, or onto an occupied slot to swap the two apps; slot numbers and the preview always agree.
- The visible slot count and the ring's entrance duration now sit above the ring; lowering the count only hides the later slots, and raising it brings them back.
- When the highlighted slot changes, the highlight rotates the shortest way around the ring, and a fast follow-up change continues from the position it is animating through instead of jumping back to the start.
