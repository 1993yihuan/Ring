## 新增与改进

- 选中位置的背景改成这个位置自己的环带扇区：夹在环带两条边之间，靠中孔一侧更窄、靠外缘一侧更宽，四个角都是圆的；3 到 8 个位置都不会伸到相邻位置上去。
- 配色改用环带自身描边的暖灰 #C9C3B9、62% 不透明度，取代原来的青色光斑：看起来是同一种暖米白环带深一档，下面的颗粒仍然透出来。
- 交互没有变：换到另一个位置仍是 0.05 秒渐变，图标和命中范围不动，背景也不会盖住图标。

## What's New & Improved

- The selected position's background is now that position's own sector of the band: between the band's two edges, narrower at the hole and wider at the outer edge, with rounded corners, and it stays within one position at 3 to 8 positions.
- It uses the band's own bezel grey, #C9C3B9 at 62% opacity, instead of the old cyan wash: the same warm off-white band a shade deeper, with the grain still showing through.
- Interaction is unchanged: moving to another position is still a 0.05 second fade, icons and hit areas never move, and the background never covers an icon.
