## 新增与改进

- 添加或更换应用时，可搜索正在运行或已安装的应用，用方向键和回车选择，也可浏览其他位置的 `.app`。
- 在环形设置的预览中，点击空槽的「＋」即可添加应用。将鼠标移到已有应用上，即可替换或原位移除。
- 将预览中的应用拖到另一个已有应用的槽位，两个应用就会交换位置。预览和槽位列表会同步更新，布局会自动保存。

## What's New & Improved

- When adding or replacing an app, search running or installed apps, select one with the arrow keys and Return, or browse for an `.app` in another location.
- In the ring preview in Settings, click the plus sign in an empty slot to add an app. Hover over an app to replace it or clear its slot.
- Drag an app in the preview onto another occupied slot to swap the two apps. The preview and slot list stay in sync, and Ring saves the layout automatically.
