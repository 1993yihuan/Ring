## 新增与改进

- 首次发布：连按同一侧 Option 两次，就在鼠标位置弹出环形启动器。
- 圆环有 8 个可配置的位置，点击图标会在前台打开对应的应用。
- 在设置窗口里为每个位置指定应用，改动立即保存，重启后仍然有效。
- 只占菜单栏，不占 Dock；菜单栏可以手动打开圆环，并显示输入监控权限状态、按需请求授权。
- 一份 Universal 2 安装包，Intel 与 Apple 芯片的 Mac 都能装。

## What's New & Improved

- First release: press Option twice on the same side and the ring launcher opens at the pointer.
- The ring has eight configurable positions; click an icon to bring that app to the front.
- Choose the app for each position in the settings window; changes save at once and survive a restart.
- It sits in the menu bar, not the Dock; the menu bar opens the ring by hand and shows the Input Monitoring status and asks for it on request.
- One Universal 2 build installs on both Intel and Apple silicon Macs.
