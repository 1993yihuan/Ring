## 新增与改进

- 关于页有可用更新时，「更新」按钮现在就在「Ring」标题右侧同一行，不再落在长篇更新说明中间，也不会被说明挤走。
- 更新说明很长时改为独立滚动区域，最高 180 点：说明再长也压不到标题、按钮和底部的安全提示。

## What's New & Improved

- When an update is available, the "Update" button now sits on the same line, right of the "Ring" title, instead of inside the release notes, and nothing in the notes can push it away.
- Long release notes now scroll in their own area, capped at 180 points, so they cannot cover the title, the button or the safety note below.
