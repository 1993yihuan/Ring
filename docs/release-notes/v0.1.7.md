## 新增与改进

- 设置窗口改为侧边栏分页：左侧是系统原生的侧边栏列表，固定 200 点宽，右侧显示当前页。
- 五页顺序固定为环形设置、触发与手势、权限与安全、通用设置、关于，打开时停在环形设置；环形设置页原来的内容和功能保持不变。
- 侧边栏底部显示当前版本，号码从正在运行的应用读取。
- 圆环改为从 12 点方向顺时针扫显：材质、投影、图标和空位随扫过的边界逐步揭开，图标位置不变，环心名称始终完整显示。
- 展开时长可配置，范围 0 到 2000 毫秒，默认 140 毫秒，填 0 就立即出现；空、带符号、小数、指数和超出范围的值都判为无效。
- 换到另一个高亮位置是 0.05 秒渐变，环心名称立即更新；取消或启动后整幅 0.06 秒淡出。
- 系统开启「减弱动态效果」时，显示、切换和消失都立即完成。
- 其余四页仍是「待开发」占位，还没有对应功能。

## What's New & Improved

- The settings window is now a sidebar: the native sidebar list on the left at a fixed 200 points, the current page on the right.
- The five pages keep a fixed order: ring, trigger and gestures, permissions and security, general, about. The window opens on the ring page, whose content and behaviour are unchanged.
- The current version shows at the bottom of the sidebar, read from the running app.
- The ring now appears by sweeping clockwise from 12 o'clock: the material, shadows, icons and empty positions are uncovered along the edge, icons never move, and the centre name stays complete.
- The entrance duration is configurable from 0 to 2000 milliseconds, default 140, and 0 shows the ring at once; empty, signed, fractional, exponent and out-of-range values are all rejected.
- Moving the highlight is a 0.05 second fade with the centre name updating at once, and cancelling or launching fades the whole picture out over 0.06 seconds.
- With Reduce Motion on, appearing, moving and disappearing all finish immediately.
- The other four pages are still "coming soon" placeholders with no features yet.
