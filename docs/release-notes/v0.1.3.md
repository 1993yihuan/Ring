## 新增与改进

- 可见位置数可以调成 3 到 8 个：调小时只隐藏排在后面的位置，调大时原来的配置还在。
- 设置窗口的方位预览和位置列表随位置数一起变，编号从顶部第 1 位顺时针数。
- 产品官网 https://1993yihuan.github.io/Ring/ 上线，展示圆环外观与下载入口。

## What's New & Improved

- The number of visible positions is configurable from 3 to 8: shrinking only hides the trailing positions, and growing brings the earlier configuration back.
- The settings direction preview and the position list follow that number, numbered from 1 at the top clockwise.
- The product site at https://1993yihuan.github.io/Ring/ is live, with the ring's look and a download entry point.
