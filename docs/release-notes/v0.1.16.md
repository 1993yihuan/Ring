## 新增与改进

- 选中应用时环心留空：不再显示应用名和按钮提示。松开 Option 启动选中的应用。
- 修好菜单栏图标的右键菜单。右键只弹出「设置…」和「退出 Ring」两项；左键直接打开设置，已有设置窗口会移到前台。
- 去掉「手动打开环形」和「重试全局监听」两个菜单项。Option 监听不可用时，退出 Ring 再重新打开。

## What's New & Improved

- The middle of the ring stays empty for a chosen app: no app name, no button hint. Release Option to launch the selected app.
- The menu bar icon's right-click menu works again. A right click offers only "Settings…" and "Quit Ring"; a left click opens Settings directly and brings an existing Settings window to the front.
- "Open Ring manually" and "Retry global listener" are gone from the menu. If the Option listener is unavailable, quit Ring and open it again.
