## 新增与改进

- 设置新增「触发与手势」：单选双击 Option（默认）、双击 Command 或自定义组合键，选中立即生效并保存，重启后继续使用。
- 自定义组合键是一个修饰键加一个普通键，修饰键可以是 Command、Control、Option 或 Shift 中的一个。同时按住这两个键，圆环在鼠标位置出现；先松开任意一个键，就启动当时指着的应用，没选中就只是关闭。
- 系统占用或已被其他应用注册的组合会被拒绝，Ring 显示系统返回的原因并保留原来能用的触发。旧版本保存的多修饰键组合启动时临时改用双击 Option 并提示重新录制，原 trigger.json 不改写。
- 用 Shift 或 Option 加字母时要知道：Shift 加字母本来就是大写输入，Option 加字母可能产生特殊字符或参与输入法组合，这个组合被 Ring 占用后那些输入可能不再照原样出现。注册成功只表示系统接受了这个组合，不保证每个应用都会送达。
- 双击 Command 时系统可能显示「键入以使用 Siri」提示，这是系统行为，Ring 不改变它。

## What's New & Improved

- Settings has a new Triggers & Gestures page: choose double-tap Option (the default), double-tap Command, or a custom combination. The choice applies at once, is saved, and survives a restart.
- A custom combination is one modifier plus one ordinary key, where the modifier is Command, Control, Option or Shift. Hold both keys together and the ring appears at the pointer; release either key to launch the app you are pointing at, or to just close the ring when nothing is selected.
- A combination the system owns, or one another app already registered, is refused: Ring shows the reason the system returned and keeps the trigger that worked. A multi-modifier combination saved by an older version falls back to double-tap Option at launch with a prompt to record again; the existing trigger.json is left as it is.
- Shift plus a letter is uppercase typing, and Option plus a letter may produce a special character or join an input-method composition. Once Ring holds such a combination, that input may stop appearing as it did. A successful registration only means the system accepted the combination, not that every app receives it.
- Double-tap Command may make the system show "Type to use Siri". That is the system's own behaviour; Ring does not change it.
