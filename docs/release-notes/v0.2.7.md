## 新增与改进

- 环形设置页在最小窗口（800 × 560）下不再把「可见位置数」截断为省略号：左侧标签按内容占宽，右侧「展开时长」滑块随窗口弹性伸缩；圆环预览与提示行在控制区与底栏之间上下居中。
- 「环形设置」「触发与手势」「通用设置」三页的顶部标题基线统一，切换侧边栏时标题不再上下跳动。
- 「触发与手势」页视觉与「通用设置」对齐：字号层级统一，三种启动方式改为圆角可选行（选中淡蓝底色与描边，未选中支持悬停反馈），「自定义组合键」精简为单行说明并把补充提示收进圆形问号悬浮提示，快捷键状态改用键帽与低对比度胶囊展示、录制中高亮显示。
- 配置弹窗细节打磨：应用页的键盘选中项增加极淡蓝底色与描边、底部加入柔和白渐隐；网址页「打开方式」改为通栏菜单按钮，右缘与输入框严格对齐；快捷功能页「锁定屏幕」补齐悬停反馈，「选择…」改为与其他按钮一致的轻量蓝色胶囊样式。三页底栏统一为同一高度。
- 环形设置预览更贴合桌面圆环：未选中槽位时环心显示「点击槽位编辑」「拖拽可调换顺序」低对比度引导（播放预览动画时自动隐藏），「更换 / 移除」按钮补齐悬停反馈并垂直居中；空槽由虚线方框升级为圆形凹槽加居中加号，键盘焦点轮廓同步为圆形。

## What's New & Improved

- In the Ring settings page the "Visible positions" value is no longer truncated to an ellipsis at the minimum 800 × 560 window: the label takes its natural width and the entrance-duration slider flexes with the window, and the ring preview with its hint line is now vertically centered between the controls and the footer.
- The Ring, Triggers & Gestures and General settings pages now share one top title baseline, so switching sections no longer makes the title jump.
- The Triggers & Gestures page matches General settings: consistent type scale, the three launch modes as rounded selectable rows (blue tint and outline when selected, hover feedback when not), a one-line summary for the custom shortcut with the extra notes moved into a round question-mark tooltip, and shortcut state shown with keycaps and a low-contrast capsule that highlights while recording.
- Polish in the slot picker: keyboard selection on the Apps tab gains a faint blue tint and outline plus a soft white fade at the bottom, the Websites tab "Open with" control is a full-width menu aligned to the input field's right edge, and on the Quick Actions tab Lock Screen now has hover feedback while "Choose…" uses the same light blue capsule style as the other buttons. All three tabs share one footer height.
- The Ring settings preview now matches the real ring: with no slot selected the ring center shows a low-contrast "Click a slot to edit" / "Drag to reorder" hint, hidden during the preview animation, the Replace and Remove buttons have hover feedback and sit vertically centered, and empty slots are drawn as a circular well with a centered plus sign whose focus outline is round too.
