## 新增与改进

- 启动方式改为按住选择、松开启动：连按同一侧 Option 两次，第二次按住时移动鼠标选方向，松开就启动；菜单栏仍可手动打开圆环点选。
- 圆环改为暖米白外观，默认尺寸缩小一半；出现、关闭和选中都即时响应，没有动画。
- 环心说明精简：去掉「松开取消」「Esc 取消」文案，应用名在环心居中显示；按 Esc、停在空隙或空位上仍然取消。
- 修复安装到「应用程序」后位置保存不下来的问题：配置改存到 ~/Library/Application Support/Ring，移动或重装应用都不影响。
- 修复设置窗口过大、内容被遮挡又无法缩小的问题，窗口现在可以正常调整。
- 项目改用 MIT 许可，继续免费使用、源码公开。

## What's New & Improved

- Choosing now means holding: press Option twice on the same side, keep the second press down, move the pointer to a direction, and release to launch. The menu bar still opens the ring for clicking.
- The ring now uses a warm off-white look at half its former default size, and opening, closing and highlighting respond at once with no animation.
- The centre text is simpler: the "release to cancel" and "Esc to cancel" lines are gone and the app name is centred. Esc, the gaps and an empty position still cancel.
- Fixed positions not being saved once the app was installed in Applications: the configuration now lives in ~/Library/Application Support/Ring, so moving or reinstalling the app keeps it.
- Fixed the settings window opening too large, hiding content and refusing to shrink; it now resizes normally.
- The project is now MIT licensed and stays free with public source.
