## 新增与改进

- 修复应用 A 启动期间再次唤出圆环选择应用 B 时的前台焦点问题：目标应用 B 现在可以正常打开或切至前台（包括隐藏状态下的已有窗口重开），被取代的慢启动应用 A 在完成初始化及短宽限期内不会再轻易将前台焦点抢回，也不会污染“切回上一个应用”历史。
- 优化重叠冷启动与常规切窗的交接体验：新的冷启动仅在就绪后激活最新选中的应用，减少在途慢启动对当前焦点的干扰；一旦你切换到其他应用、再次唤出圆环，或待被取代应用初始化结束并度过短宽限期，后续通过程序坞等正常方式切回该应用不会受到拦截。

## What's New & Improved

- Fixed foreground focus handling when summoning the ring to select app B while app A is still starting up: target app B now opens or comes to the front reliably (including reopening hidden or windowless running apps), and a slow superseded launch of A is prevented from stealing focus back or polluting the "switch to previous app" history during its startup lifecycle and brief grace period.
- Improved handoff between overlapping cold launches and normal app switching: new cold launches activate the latest selected app upon completion to reduce background interference, while switching to another app, reopening the ring, or returning to A after its initialization and brief grace window finishes works normally.
