## 新增与改进

- 鼠标方向决定高亮哪个位置，环心显示这个位置上的应用名。
- 在圆环外、同一块屏幕的任意位置单击，就按方向启动高亮的应用；单击环内的图标启动它自己。
- 点环心、空位或图标之间的空隙只做方向高亮，单击即取消；其他屏幕上的点击一律不启动。
- 新增位置编号：顶部是第 1 位，顺时针排到最后一位，设置窗口的方位预览和列表用同一套编号。
- 监听失败不再被当成没授权：权限提示把「没有授权」和「监听起不来」分开，设置窗口可以直接重试监听，也能跳到系统设置去授予输入监控。
- 安装包按架构分成两个 DMG：Intel 用 intel，Apple 芯片用 apple-silicon。

## What's New & Improved

- The pointer's direction picks the highlighted position, and the centre of the ring names the app there.
- A click anywhere outside the ring on the same screen launches the highlighted app; a click on an icon inside launches that app.
- The centre, an empty position, or the gap between icons only highlights by direction and cancels on click; clicks on other screens never launch anything.
- Positions are numbered from 1 at the top clockwise to the last one, and the settings direction preview and list share that numbering.
- A listener that fails is no longer shown as missing permission; the permission notice separates "not authorized" from "could not start", and the settings window can retry the listener or open System Settings to grant Input Monitoring.
- Installs ship as two architecture-specific DMGs: intel for Intel Macs, apple-silicon for Apple silicon.
